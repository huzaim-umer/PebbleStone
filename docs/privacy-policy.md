# Privacy Policy for GemLeap

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

This privacy policy explains how the GemLeap app ("we", "the app") handles
information. By using GemLeap you agree to this policy.

## Information the app collects directly
GemLeap does **not** require an account and does **not** collect personal
information that identifies you. Your game progress and statistics (games
played, win streaks, settings, and the game you are currently playing) are stored
**only on your device**. The one exception is the optional global leaderboard
described below.

## Information collected by third parties
GemLeap uses the following third-party services, which may collect information
as described in their own privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob** (advertising). To show ads, AdMob may access your device's
  advertising identifier and collect information such as approximate usage and
  device data. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and
  https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
- **Google Play Billing** (in-app purchases). If you buy "Remove Ads", the
  transaction is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your
  payment details.
- **Google Play Games Services** (global leaderboard). If you sign in to Google
  Play Games, your total number of wins is submitted to Google and shown on the
  leaderboard together with your Play Games profile name and avatar, so other
  players can see them. Google also uses your Play Games player ID to keep your
  score and rank. Signing in is optional and the game works fully without it. We
  do not receive your name, email address or any other account details. See
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Play** (app updates). The app asks Google Play whether a newer version
  is available and can offer to install it. No personal information is sent to us.

For users in regions requiring it (e.g. the EEA/UK), the app presents a consent
prompt (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting personalised ads.

## Children
GemLeap is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under
13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention and deletion
On-device data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. For
data held by Google (ads/purchases), see Google's policies linked above. Your
leaderboard score is held by Google Play Games Services; you can delete your Play
Games data (including your leaderboard entry) from the Google Play Games app under
Settings, or by contacting us at the address below.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected
by updating the "Last updated" date above.
