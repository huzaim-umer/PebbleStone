GemLeap

A calm, classic marble solitaire puzzle for Android.

Jump the gemstones over each other into empty holes and clear the board down to a single stone. No timers, no pressure - just a satisfying brain-teaser you can pick up any time.

Features

  • Classic 33-hole wooden board with polished gemstone marbles
  • A new Daily Challenge every day, with win streaks
  • Endless free play, free hints, and undo
  • Stats, smooth animations, and full offline play
